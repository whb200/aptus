The labor market didn’t weaken. It slowed.

Hiring, quits, and job changes have fallen to multi-decade lows even as employment has held. The market still clears — just more slowly, and for fewer people, than at any point in recent memory.

Aptus Research studies labor dynamism — the pace at which workers and organizations find each other — and what a slow-clearing market means for companies, their leaders, and the people who power them.

Method

We build predictive models of labor-market behavior and pair them with fieldwork inside the working economy. The models tell us what is happening. The fieldwork tells us what it costs.

Current work

A long-form examination of the modern labor market and the people inside it is underway.
